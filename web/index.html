Wi-Fi network

Use your home Wi-Fi or your phone’s hotspot. Your phone keeps internet access, and the badge reconnects automatically. Use a 2.4 GHz network.

For a phone hotspot: save its name and password here, leave the badge Wi-Fi, then enable your hotspot. Hold the badge screen and choose “Connect to editor” to scan its new address. On iPhone, enable “Maximize Compatibility”.

Leave the name empty to forget the saved network.
